Before we start.

Can we commission one service?

Yes. Advisory, authorised testing, training or an exercise can stand alone. Existing evidence and any gaps are considered when defining the scope.

How are fees agreed?

You receive a proposal setting out the work, deliverables, limits and fees before you commit. The scope reflects the question, sites, authority and specialist requirements.

Can you work with our team and suppliers?

Yes. Independent advice can improve the use of existing measures and identify justified changes. Palisade retains its advisory independence.

How is testing managed around live operations?

Timing, permissions, exclusions, safety limits and stop conditions are agreed in advance. Potential operational impact is considered explicitly in the scope.

What should we share initially?

Your sector, broad location and the question you need answered. Confidentiality arrangements and a suitable channel are agreed before detailed site information is exchanged.
