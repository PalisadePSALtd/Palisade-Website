Risk before recommendations

Understand the system.
Set the priorities.

We assess your assets, credible threats, vulnerabilities and critical dependencies. People, physical measures, information and procedures are considered in the context of how the organisation operates.

Advice can strengthen existing controls, define new requirements or support implementation. It follows assessed risk and operational need.

An on-site review can consider boundaries, access arrangements, personnel practices and important dependencies. Observations are assessed alongside available documentation, the threat picture and explicit evidence limits.

Threat & vulnerability assessment

Establish the assets and consequences that matter, assess credible threats and identify exposure. Assumptions and assessment limits are explicit.

Protective-security reviews

Review physical measures, personnel practices, information exposure, access arrangements and the connections between them.

Operational requirements & planning

Define the protective outcome, performance needed and responsibilities before specifying products or changes.

Independent implementation advice

Prioritise investment, assess proposed solutions against requirements and define how improvement should be verified.

What you receive

A decision-focused assessment, prioritised improvement roadmap and senior debrief. Requirements, implementation support and verification criteria are agreed in scope.
