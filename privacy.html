How personal information is used when you visit this website or contact Palisade.

Palisade / Winchester · UK wide

Privacy & personal information / 6 October 2026

This notice explains how Palisade Protective Security Advisory Ltd uses personal information in connection with this website, business enquiries and the commissioning of our services.

Who is responsible

Palisade Protective Security Advisory Ltd is the controller of personal information we receive for these purposes. We are registered in England and Wales, company number 17268524, at 47 Southgate Street, Winchester, Hampshire, SO23 9EH.

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email info@palisadeprotectivesecurity.co.uk or write to the address above.

Information we receive

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, work contact details, organisation, role, broad requirement and correspondence. Further information may be needed to prepare a proposal or deliver an agreed engagement. Please do not send detailed vulnerabilities, site drawings, sensitive operational information or unnecessary personal information in an initial enquiry.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. Hosting also involves processing the requests needed to deliver the pages and assets. This is separate from the enquiry builder.

How the enquiry builder works

The enquiry builder assembles a draft locally in your browser and asks your email application to open it. It does not submit your entries to Palisade or a form-processing service. We receive them only if you send the email. Your email provider handles the draft and message according to its own arrangements.

Why we use information

We use business contact information to respond to enquiries, establish fit, prepare proposals, manage professional relationships and organise agreed work. Our legitimate interests are running an independent advisory practice and responding to people who approach us about its services. Where information is necessary to enter into or perform a contract with you personally, we rely on that contractual basis.

We may also use information to meet legal obligations, maintain necessary business records and establish, exercise or defend legal claims. We do not use this website to make decisions about you solely through automated processing.

Who may receive it

GitHub Pages provides website hosting and Proton provides Palisade’s email service. Other providers may support document storage and business administration where needed. Professional advisers and associates may receive relevant information where needed for an agreed engagement. We limit sharing to the purpose and applicable confidentiality arrangements. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

GitHub and Proton may process information outside the UK. Proton’s published Mail policy describes message storage in Switzerland, Germany or Norway. Provider details are available in the GitHub privacy statement and Proton Mail privacy policy. Where we arrange a restricted international transfer, it must be covered by an applicable adequacy arrangement or another permitted safeguard. Contact us for information about the arrangements relevant to your enquiry or engagement.

How long information is kept

Enquiry correspondence is kept while the request and any necessary follow-up are being dealt with. If an engagement does not follow, retention depends on whether there is an ongoing business purpose or a specific need to retain correspondence for legal claims. Information that no longer serves those purposes should be deleted or anonymised.

Where work follows, the retention period depends on delivery and continuing-assurance needs, contractual requirements, applicable accounting and other legal obligations, and relevant limitation periods. These criteria apply to the record concerned; they do not justify keeping unrelated information indefinitely. You can ask us about the retention arrangements applicable to your information.

Cookies and tracking

The website code does not set cookies or use analytics, advertising trackers, browser storage or third-party form processing. The site uses locally hosted assets. GitHub Pages still logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described above. If tracking or other data-collection features are introduced, the notice and any required choices must be updated.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access your information, correct inaccuracies, request erasure, restrict processing and receive portable information. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it. Rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable data-protection law.

Your right to object

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. You have an absolute right to object to use of your information for direct marketing.

Contact us using the details above. We may need proportionate information to verify your identity. You may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. You do not have to contact us first, although we welcome the opportunity to address a concern.

Engagement-specific information

This notice concerns the website and general business relationship. Work involving additional categories of personal information, particular sharing arrangements or client-controlled information may require engagement-specific privacy information and contractual arrangements before processing starts.

Changes to this notice

The date above identifies this version. We will update the notice when the relevant purposes or arrangements change and provide additional information where required.
