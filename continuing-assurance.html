05 / Continuing assurance

Confidence that
develops over time.

Protection changes with your organisation. A continuing relationship keeps the risk picture, improvement priorities and evidence current.

A continuing relationship

Review the change.
Verify the improvement.

A programme follows an initial review or sufficient existing evidence. Cadence and scope reflect your sites, operations, changes and required assurance.

Review, advice, learning and targeted verification are combined into agreed work packages. Renewal follows the value of the evidence and progress achieved.

01Review02Improve03Exercise04Assure05Remediate06Retest

01 / Review

What has changed?

Update important assets, exposure and dependencies. Consider changes in the organisation, operating arrangements and threat assumptions.

Useful output

A current risk picture and priorities for the programme.

02 / Improve

What should improve first?

Agree proportionate actions and the protective outcome required. Maintain owners, priorities and practical milestones.

Useful output

An improvement roadmap with clear responsibilities.

03 / Exercise

Are people and plans ready?

Examine decisions, communication and response through agreed tabletop or live exercises. Use the findings to target further learning.

Useful output

Lessons, action owners and readiness improvements.

04 / Assure

Do the selected controls perform?

Use targeted assurance, including authorised physical penetration testing where appropriate, to answer defined protective questions within agreed limits.

Useful output

Evidence of performance, with explicit scope and assessment limits.

05 / Remediate

How will findings be addressed?

Assign improvement actions and support implementation where agreed. Set the evidence and criteria needed to verify the change.

Useful output

A practical remediation plan and verification criteria.

06 / Retest

Has the relevant weakness been addressed?

Recheck material findings against the original protective requirement. Review remaining exposure and agree the next assurance priorities.

Useful output

Verified changes and decisions for continuing review.

Scope follows your question and available evidence. Changes can trigger targeted review or verification at any stage.

Update exposure & priorities

Scheduled or risk-triggered reviews consider changed threats, sites, personnel, dependencies and operating arrangements.

Track the improvement programme

Maintain owners, milestones and a practical roadmap, including advice on implementation where agreed.

Exercise & verify selectively

Test readiness and selected assumptions. Targeted retesting checks whether material findings have been addressed.

Report for decisions

Provide leadership with progress, remaining exposure and priorities for the next review cycle.

Clear scope and cadence

Define included activities, deliverables and review triggers. New sites or major changes can require a revised scope; the programme earns its continuation through useful decisions and follow-through.
