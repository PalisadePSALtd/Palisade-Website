Controlled verification

Test the assumption.
Establish the evidence.

Physical penetration testing examines agreed access controls, protective layers and routes to critical assets within written authority. Detection, escalation and response are assessed alongside access.

Red-team scenarios can extend the question across people, procedures and technical interfaces. Activity and specialist input follow the authorised scope.

Agree the control or protective outcome that needs evidence before selecting a test. Written authority, operating limits and suitable personnel define the engagement; findings support improvement and retesting.

Define the protective question

Identify the control, asset or operational outcome to be examined. Use existing assessment evidence or establish the gaps before testing.

Agree authority & operating limits

Document permissions, exclusions, safety arrangements, timing, operational impact and stop conditions before activity starts.

Capture evidence & consequences

Record findings with appropriate supporting evidence. Separate demonstrated weaknesses, assumptions and areas that remain untested.

Remediate & retest

Prioritise improvement actions and verify relevant changes against the original finding and protective requirement.
