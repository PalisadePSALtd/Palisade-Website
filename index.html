An Independent Specialist Practice.

Palisade is founded and led by a Royal Marines Commando Major (Retd.) — ASIS Certified Protection Professional (CPP), Member of the Security Institute (MSyI), NPSA-endorsed SFJ Level 4 Protective Security Adviser, and Developed Vetting (DV) cleared. The combination of these credentials is uncommon in the independent market; together they evidence both the professional standing and the formal endorsement required for engagements at Critical National Infrastructure, government, and HNW principal level.

All work is structured within the NPSA Protective Security Risk Management framework and aligned to the four converged pillars — physical, personnel, technical, and cyber. Engagements address the full threat spectrum in NPSA PSRM guidance and the UK NSRA: terrorism, hostile state espionage and reconnaissance, insider threat, transnational repression of diaspora, serious and organised crime, and cyber-physical convergent attacks.

Russian-language capability. Hostile state activity and transnational repression engagements are supported directly in Russian where required — enabling clearer client liaison and more accurate threat characterisation without intermediary translation.

Martyn's Law — Statutory Duty, Proportionate Response.

The Terrorism (Protection of Premises) Act 2025 — SIA regulated, enforcement Spring 2027 — places new statutory duties on qualifying premises and events. Public protection documentation must be aligned to Home Office statutory guidance (April 2026).

Palisade delivers Martyn's Law engagements formatted for SIA compliance submission, structured through the NPSA Operational Requirements process so measures remain proportionate to risk — not over-engineered, and not under-prepared.

Our Methodology

A Five-Phase NPSA-Aligned Methodology

Every Palisade engagement follows a structured methodology aligned to the NPSA Protective Security Risk Management lifecycle. ISO 31000 risk management applied throughout; all work conducted under written scope and authorisation.

1
Asset & Threat
Desktop OSINT and threat profiling to establish asset criticality and adversarial context.
2
Reconnaissance

Risk & OR

4
Testing

5
Reporting
