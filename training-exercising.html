Tailored to roles and risk

Recognise.
Decide. Act.

Security teams, front-line staff, sensitive personnel and leaders have different responsibilities. Agree the audience, learning objectives and format before delivery.

Training develops understanding and practice. Exercises examine whether plans, communication and decisions work together.

Build role-specific recognition, decisions and reporting through practical learning. The audience, objectives and responsibilities shape the programme.

Protective-security & counter-terrorism awareness

Connect everyday responsibilities to access, visitor handling, information protection and incident procedures.

Hostile-reconnaissance awareness

Recognise concerning observation or information-gathering and practise appropriate reporting and escalation.

Anti-surveillance training

Build awareness of surveillance exposure and proportionate protective behaviour around sensitive work, visits and travel. Tailored learning can consider arrival arrangements and everyday working practices; audience, role boundaries and appropriate reporting are agreed first.

Counter-surveillance preparedness

Develop role-specific understanding, co-ordination and reporting for designated teams. Specialist depth and delivery personnel are agreed in scope.

Security-team development

Exercise observation, verification, escalation, control-room interfaces and response against site requirements.

Tabletop & live exercises

Examine plans, command decisions, communication and response using agreed scenarios. Complexity follows the operational requirement.

Learning that becomes improvement

Capture lessons, assign action owners and define follow-up criteria. Further training or assurance should address the findings, rather than repeat a standard package.
