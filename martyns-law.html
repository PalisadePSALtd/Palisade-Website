Martyn’s Law.
Preparedness that works.

Support for complex premises and events: understand the requirement, review arrangements and develop usable preparedness.

Palisade / Winchester · UK wide

Proportionate support

Clear responsibilities.
Practical readiness.

The Terrorism (Protection of Premises) Act 2025 sets duties for qualifying premises and events. Official guidance should inform assessment of your scope and requirements.

The responsible person retains legal accountability. Independent advice supports their decisions; it does not provide a compliance certificate.

Review preparedness

Examine public protection procedures, responsibilities, vulnerabilities and the people who must act.

Improve usable arrangements

Prioritise proportionate changes to procedures, communication and protective measures where required.

Develop & exercise readiness

Support targeted learning and exercise plans; capture lessons and follow-up actions.

Support complex requirements

Agree assistance for multi-site or enhanced-tier arrangements and documentation according to the actual requirement.
